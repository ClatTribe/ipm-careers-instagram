# Writing rules for IPM Careers posts and Stories
- English. Warm, peer-mentor tone. Short lines. No corporate words, no exclamation-mark spam.
- **No em dashes or en dashes** anywhere (slides, captions). `tools/check.mjs` blocks them.
- Write **IIM B UGAT** (not "IIM B UG"). IPMAT Indore, IPMAT Rohtak and JIPMAT are different exams; never mix them.
- **No invented numbers, ranks, results or dates.** Every fact goes in `claims` with its source. A source that starts
  with `VERIFY` blocks posting until a person checks it.
- PYQs: only questions marked verified in the portal bank; the answer must match the bank.
- Portal features: describe only what the live page shows. Screenshots use dummy data; no real student names or scores.
  Never claim a reply time for doubts.
- Never name competitors. Never promise a seat, score or result.
- Captions: at most 5 hashtags; one call to action (study.ipmcareer.com, or WhatsApp wa.me/918299470392).
- Weekly formats: Mon checklist, Tue solve in 60 sec, Wed inside the portal, Thu myth vs fact, Fri word of the day,
  Sat shortcut card, Sun for parents. Copy an existing post of the same format.
